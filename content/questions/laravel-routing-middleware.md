## Why This Is Asked
Routing and middleware determine how Laravel receives, validates, protects, and responds to HTTP requests.
## Key Concepts
- Routes map HTTP methods and paths to controllers or closures.
- Middleware performs cross-cutting work around a request.
- Route groups share prefixes, names, and middleware.
- Middleware order affects authentication, bindings, and responses.
