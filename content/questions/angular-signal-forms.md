## Why This Is Asked

With Signal Forms becoming stable in Angular 22, understanding the shift from the traditional `FormControl/FormGroup` (Reactive Forms) to a signal-based approach is essential. Interviewers want to see if you understand the benefits of fine-grained reactivity in forms, improved type safety, and the reduced boilerplate that Signal Forms provide.

## Key Concepts

- **Signal-based Reactivity:** Forms that update only what is necessary.
- **Improved Type Safety:** Better inference and stricter checking than traditional forms.
- **Boilerplate Reduction:** Simpler declaration and validation syntax.
- **Interoperability:** How Signal Forms can co-exist with or migrate from Reactive Forms.
