## Why This Is Asked

The OWASP Top 10 is a common baseline for discussing application-security priorities. Interviewers want candidates to recognize the major risk classes, translate them into concrete engineering controls, and avoid treating a checklist as complete threat modeling.

## Key Concepts

- **Current edition:** The 2025 list is the current OWASP Top 10 edition.
- **Access control:** Authorization must be enforced server-side on every sensitive action.
- **Secure delivery:** Configuration, dependency supply chain, logging, and error handling are security concerns.
- **Defense in depth:** Threat modeling, testing, monitoring, and remediation complement the Top 10.
